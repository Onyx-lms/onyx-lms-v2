Beyond the briefNine things nobody asked for

These appear nowhere in the twenty-five requirements. They were built while getting those right, and they are in the product today.

#KindWhat it is
01PlatformA whole tier above your institution
A separate operator console that can create, suspend or remove an entire institution — and, when a customer wants it, run theirs for them without them ever signing in. Seven institutions live on it today.
02PlatformEach institution runs its own front desk
Whether learners may sign themselves up, which email domains are accepted, the community link on the jobs page — the institution changes these itself instead of raising a ticket with us.
03PlatformA second, platform-wide paper trail
Separate from each institution's own log, everything a platform operator does across every institution is recorded — including simply opening somebody's grades.
04CommerceA storefront, not just fee collection
Courses and live-class programmes sold directly, a public marketing site with its own catalogue, cart, checkout, coupons and invoices — revenue tracked apart from institutional fees.
05EngineeringA build that fails on the mistake this product made most
The commonest defect found was a screen quietly showing a wrong number once an institution grew — 20 enrolments displayed where there were 1,441. That exact shape of mistake now breaks the build the moment anyone writes it again.
06EngineeringA technical reference that cannot lie about itself
The document listing every endpoint and whether it is protected is generated from the code itself. A guard it cannot read fails the build rather than being published as safe.
07Engineering339 checks that run against the deployed product
Twenty-one suites signing in as operator, administrator, examinations officer, lecturer, placement officer, employer, guardian and student, exercising real journeys against the live site — not a mock of it.
08DesignAccessibility checked with tools, not opinions
Every core screen swept with axe-core for contrast, control names, headings and keyboard reach — in light mode and again in dark mode, which returned no violations at all.
09DesignA dark theme across the entire product

01

Browse and join courses

Every course the institution offers, in one place. Depending on how a course is set up a learner joins it instantly, pays to unlock it, or is added by the institution — all three routes were used during this check.

Video, reading and document lessons

A course can mix video, documents, images, links and written pages. Every kind of lesson — not just video — remembers exactly where a learner stopped, so moving from a phone to a laptop carries on from the same line.

03

Attendance, by QR code or by hand

The teacher opens a session and a code goes on the projector. Learners scan it with their own phone — no app. The code redraws itself every 15 seconds, so a photograph sent to a friend outside the room is dead within half a minute. The register can also be marked by hand, and every learner carries a running percentage.

Working

04

Homework: set it, mark it, hand it back

Work is set with its marking guide attached, handed in online, and comes back with a score and written feedback. Late work is handled by the institution's own rule rather than by the teacher remembering it.

One screen: what to do next
