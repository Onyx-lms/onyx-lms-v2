About the role

Octopus Energy's platform team builds the systems that price, bill and forecast energy for six million households. Graduates join a squad of five to eight engineers on their first day and ship to production in their first fortnight — there is no rotation programme and no shadowing period.

You will be paired with a senior engineer for your first six months, and the first quarter is deliberately weighted towards reading and testing existing systems rather than writing new ones.

What you would do

  • Build and maintain services in Python and Go behind the customer billing platform.
  • Take a feature from a written brief through to a production release, including its tests.
  • Sit on the support rota one week in six, with a senior engineer alongside you.
  • Review other people's changes, and have yours reviewed, every working day.

What they are asking for

  • A degree in computing, engineering, mathematics or physics, graduating in 2027.
  • Working knowledge of one general-purpose language — the team does not mind which.
  • SQL beyond SELECT *: joins, grouping and an idea of what an index is for.
  • Evidence you have finished something. A coursework project counts.
  • The right to work in the UK. Octopus does not sponsor visas for this intake.
